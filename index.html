Research interest

I am interested in the representation theory of reductive groups and its applications to algebraic geometry, harmonic analysis and number theory. Most recently, my research has focused on the study of generalized Whittaker models associated to nilpotent orbits, and on the spectral decomposition of certain homogeneous spaces.
